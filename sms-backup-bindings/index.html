Changing phones is when it hits you

Verification codes keep arriving, and you still have no idea what accounts are tied to your number. Digging through old SMS feels like archaeology. Trying to delete an account is worse—the cancel button is buried or missing.

Honest limits

This app won’t catch every verification SMS. Zero results are common if your messaging app auto-deletes OTPs (turn that off—deleted messages don’t come back) or if OTP protection blocks reads. Be honest with yourself before you blame the ledger.

So why use it anyway?

Because a local binding ledger still helps. You won’t magically find every binding on earth, but you can recover most of the important ones—and take the backup plus ledger with you when you switch phones. No cloud sync story. Local first.
